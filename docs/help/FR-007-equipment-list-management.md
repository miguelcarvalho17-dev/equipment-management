# Find and filter equipment (AppSource end-user help)

Use the equipment list to see all your equipment, search it, and narrow it down by category, status or assigned employee.

## Before you start: permissions
Everyone with **Equipment Reader** (also Manager and Administrator) can open the list, search it and filter it. Employee names in the list need the standard **D365 BASIC** permission set; without it the name column is blank.

## Open the list
Search for **Equipment** and open the page. It shows all equipment in every status. The first time you open it, a tip explains search and filters.

## Search by equipment number or serial number
Choose **Search** and type part of the equipment number or serial number. The search looks in the columns you can see. If you hide the **No.** or **Serial No.** column, you can no longer search by that value.

## Filter the list
Open the filter pane and add a filter on the field you want:
- **Category Code**
- **Status**, or choose a status view at the top of the list (Available, Assigned, In Maintenance, Lost, Disposed)
- **Assigned Employee No.**

You can combine filters.

## See who has the equipment
**Assigned Employee Name** shows the name of the employee the equipment is assigned to. It is blank when the equipment is not assigned. You cannot filter or search on the name. Filter on **Assigned Employee No.** instead; the employee lookup shows names.

## Open equipment from a category
On **Equipment Categories**, select a category and choose **Equipment**. The list opens with only that category. A category without equipment opens an empty list.

## Open an employee's equipment
On the **Employee Card**, choose **Navigate > Assigned Equipment**. The list shows the equipment currently assigned to that employee. For past assignments, choose **Equipment Assignments** (see assignment history).

## Release notes
The equipment list now explains how to search and filter, shows the name of the assigned employee, and can be opened already filtered from a category (Equipment Categories > Equipment) and from an employee (Employee Card > Assigned Equipment).
