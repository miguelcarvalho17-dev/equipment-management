# Change the language of Equipment Management (AppSource end-user help)

Equipment Management shows its captions, ToolTips, messages and error messages in English, Portuguese (Portugal) or Spanish (Spain). The language follows the **Language** setting in Business Central.

## Change your language
1. Choose the **Settings** icon in the top right corner, and then choose **My Settings**.
2. In the **Language** field, select **Portuguese (Portugal)** or **Spanish (Spain)**.
3. Choose **OK**.
4. Sign out of Business Central and sign in again. The new language applies after you sign in.

The change affects only your own workspace. Other users keep their own language. For more, see [Change basic settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/ui-change-basic-settings#language).

## If the language is not in the list
Only the languages that your administrator has allowed appear in the **Language** list. Ask your administrator to allow **Portuguese (Portugal)** or **Spanish (Spain)** on the **Allowed Languages** page. See [Specify the languages that are available in your Business Central environment](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/admin-allowed-languages).

## What changes and what does not
- **Changes:** page and field names, ToolTips, actions, messages, error messages and teaching tips of Equipment Management.
- **Does not change:** the data you enter, such as equipment descriptions, notes and category descriptions. The default categories and number series that were created when the app was installed keep the language they were created in.
- **Does not change:** the date and number format. The format follows your **Region** setting in **My Settings**.
- **Other languages:** if your language is not Portuguese (Portugal) or Spanish (Spain), the app shows English.
- **Excel reports:** the column headings in the Excel reports are English.

Some texts may use different words than your team uses. Tell your administrator if a text is unclear.

## Release notes
