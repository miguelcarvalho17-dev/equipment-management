# Equipment dashboard (AppSource end-user help)

The equipment dashboard shows key figures and two charts about your equipment. It is the home page of the **Equipment Manager** role and is also available as a separate page.

## Before you start: permissions
Everyone with **Equipment Reader** can open the dashboard; Manager and Administrator include it. Choosing the **Equipment Manager** role (home page) does not give edit rights: what you can change depends on your permission set.

## Open the dashboard
- **Equipment Manager role:** open **My Settings**, choose **Equipment Manager** in the **Role** field, and save. The dashboard becomes your home page. An administrator can also assign the role in **Profiles (Roles)**.
- **Any other role:** search for **Equipment Dashboard**, or open the **Equipment** list and choose **Reports > Dashboard**. The first time, a tip explains the page.

## Tiles
The tiles count your equipment:
- **Total Equipment**: every equipment record, including lost and disposed equipment.
- **Available Equipment**, **Assigned Equipment**, **Equipment in Maintenance**, **Lost Equipment**, **Disposed Equipment**: equipment with that status.

Choose a tile to open the Equipment list showing only the equipment behind the number.

The **Overdue Maintenance Equipment** tile, in the group **Planned Maintenance**, counts equipment whose next maintenance date is before today (not lost or disposed equipment, not equipment without a date). It is red when at least one is overdue and green when none is. Choose it to open the Equipment list with only that equipment. See the overdue maintenance help (FR-020).

The **Maintenance Costs** tiles show **Total Maintenance Cost** and **Average Maintenance Cost** of all posted maintenance. Choose one to open the Maintenance History.

## Charts
- **Equipment by Category**: a column for each category that has equipment. Equipment without a category appears as a column without a label.
- **Equipment by Status**: a pie chart with a segment for each status.

- **Maintenance Cost by Category** and **Top 10 Equipment by Maintenance Cost**: columns with the posted maintenance cost. See the maintenance costs help.

Choose a column or a segment to open the Equipment list filtered to that category or status. For the top 10 chart, a column opens the maintenance history of that equipment.

## Refresh
The numbers are calculated when the page opens. To update them, choose **Refresh** on the dashboard, or on a chart.

## Role Center
The Equipment Manager home page also has shortcuts to **Equipment** and **Assignments**, to **Equipment Setup** and **Equipment Categories** (in the Setup section), and an action to create new equipment. It also shows the two maintenance cost charts and a **Maintenance History** shortcut.
