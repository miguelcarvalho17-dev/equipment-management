# Equipment card (AppSource end-user help)

The equipment card shows everything about one piece of equipment: its details, status, current assignment, assignment history and who created and last changed it.

## Before you start: permissions
Everyone with **Equipment Reader** can open the card and see the history. To change equipment or assign it, you need **Equipment Manager**.

## Open the card
Search for **Equipment**, then open a piece of equipment from the list. The first time you open the card, a tip explains its sections.

## Details and status
The **General** section holds the equipment details. **Status** is highlighted so you can see it at a glance.

## Assignment
- **Assigned Employee No.** and **Assigned Employee Name**: who has the equipment now.
- **Assigned Since**: the date it was assigned to that employee.
- **Assigned By**: the user who made the assignment.

These fields are blank when the equipment is not assigned. Use **Assign to Employee** and **Return** to change the assignment.

## Assignment History
This section lists every assignment of the equipment, newest first: employee, assignment date, who assigned it, status, closing date and who closed it. The current assignment is shown in green. You cannot edit the history here.

## Audit
Shows **Created Date**, **Created By**, **Last Modified Date** and **Last Modified By**. **Created By** and **Last Modified By** are blank if you do not have read access to the list of users. Ask your administrator if you need to see them.

## Release notes
The equipment card now shows who the equipment is assigned to and since when, lists the full assignment history, and shows who created and last changed the record. Created By and Last Modified By are blank for users without access to the user list.
