# Equipment maintenance (AppSource end-user help)

Register the maintenance work done on your equipment: when it was done, what kind, who did it and what it cost. The records form the maintenance history of each piece of equipment.

## Before you start: permissions
- **Equipment Reader** can view maintenance records.
- **Equipment Manager** (and Administrator) can register and correct them.
- An administrator must first set **Maintenance Nos.** in **Equipment Setup**. New companies get it automatically; in a company that already used the app, choose a number series there before the first record.

## Register maintenance
1. Open the equipment card and choose **Register Maintenance**. Or search for **Equipment Maintenance** and choose **New**.
2. Fill in **Equipment No.** (already filled when you start from the card), **Maintenance Date**, **Maintenance Type** (Preventive, Corrective, Inspection or Upgrade; new records start as Preventive) and **Description**.
3. Optionally add **Vendor No.**, **Performed By** and **Cost**. Leave the vendor blank when the work was done internally. The cost must be zero or greater.

Good to know:
- The date cannot be later than today. If you leave it blank, the work date is used.
- You cannot register maintenance for equipment that is **Lost** or **Disposed**. On the card, Register Maintenance is then unavailable.
- Registering maintenance does not change the equipment **Status**. Set it to **In Maintenance** yourself if needed.

## View maintenance
- **Equipment Maintenance** list: all records, newest number first. Use the views at the top (Preventive, Corrective, Inspection, Upgrade) to show one type. Choose **Equipment** or **Vendor** to open those cards.
- From an equipment card or the Equipment list, choose **Maintenance** (Navigate) to see that equipment's records only.
- The **Maintenance** FactBox on the equipment pages shows **No. of Maintenance Records** (choose it to open them), **Last Maintenance Date**, **Last Maintenance Type** and **Total Maintenance Cost**. The last date and type are blank when nothing is registered.

## Correct a record
You can change the date, type, vendor, cost and descriptions of a saved record as long as its **Status** is **Open**. After you choose **Post**, the record is locked (see the maintenance history help). You cannot change its **Equipment No.** or number, and you cannot delete it. Equipment that has maintenance records cannot be deleted either; set its status to **Disposed** instead.

## Equipment Manager home page
The role shows **Maintenance** in the navigation bar and **Maintenance Record** under New.

## Release notes
You can now register maintenance on equipment: date, type (preventive, corrective, inspection, upgrade), vendor, cost and who did the work. Choose Register Maintenance on the equipment card, or open the Equipment Maintenance list. A new Maintenance FactBox shows the number of records, the last maintenance and the total cost. Maintenance records cannot be deleted. In companies that already use the app, an administrator must choose Maintenance Nos. in Equipment Setup.
